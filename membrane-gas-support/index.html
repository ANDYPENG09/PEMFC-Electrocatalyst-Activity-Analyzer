01 / TEST RECORD

Select a run

Edit the selected run. Select the reference run separately when you want to edit its conditions.

1. Test setup
Enter absolute pressures and standard flows (0 °C / 1 atm). For sweep, measure total outlet flow including sweep and confirm the sweep contains neither target component.
2. Calibrated gas composition

Use calibrated dry-basis GC mole/volume % at feed, retentate and permeate; permeate fractions include the sweep gas. Verify sampling and drying do not change non-water gas amounts. Raw peak areas are not concentrations.

3. Humidity and outlet flow basis

Use measured or independently determined water partial pressures at each membrane-side location. For wet outlet flow, enter the water fraction at the flow meter. Leave unused fields blank.

Estimate water pressure from temperature and RH

Use the measured temperature and relative humidity at the specific side. The estimate is not automatically applied; copy it to the matching side only. Range: 0–69.85 °C, 0–100% RH.

4. Measurement checks

Mark the checks based on recorded evidence. Unknown or failed checks prevent calculation.

Methods & equations

For each gas i: pᵢ = (P − pH₂O) × xᵢ,dry. Driving force is feed pᵢ minus permeate pᵢ, or the mean of feed and retentate pᵢ minus permeate pᵢ. Component flow = dry total outlet flow × dry outlet mole fraction. Permeance = component molar flow / (area × driving force). For wet outlet flow, dry flow = wet flow × (1 − water mole fraction at the flow meter). Separation factor = (yA/yB)/(xA/xB). Sweep must be free of A and B, and the sampled outlet gas must represent the membrane outlet.
